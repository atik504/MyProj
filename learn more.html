Frequently Asked Questions

What video formats are supported?
VidSharp AI supports most common video formats including MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV, and WebM. If you have a specific format not listed here, please contact us for compatibility information.
Is there a limit on video length or file size?
The free version allows videos up to 5 minutes in length and 500MB in size. Premium subscribers can process videos up to 2 hours long with a 4GB file size limit.
How long does processing take?
Processing time depends on video length, resolution, and selected enhancement options. As a general guideline, a 1-minute 720p video typically takes 3-5 minutes to enhance to 4K.
Do you store my videos?
We temporarily store your videos during processing, but all content is automatically deleted within 24 hours. We never access or use your content for any purpose other than providing our enhancement service.
Can I enhance videos on my mobile device?
Yes! VidSharp AI works on any device with a modern web browser, including smartphones and tablets. We also offer dedicated iOS and Android apps for an optimized mobile experience.
